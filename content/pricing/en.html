The trial

14 days free on every plan. Nothing is charged during the trial even if a card was entered, and the card is held by the payment processor, not by us. The first charge happens when the trial ends, and it is a monthly subscription from there.

Without a payment method, access simply stops at the end of the trial. Nothing is deleted: choosing a plan restores access immediately, exactly where it left off.

Billing questions

Can I cancel?

Any time, from inside the system. There is no annual contract and no exit fee. Cancellation takes effect at the end of the period already paid for — not in the middle of a month you paid for.

What if the team grows past the cap?

When you add the employee who crosses it, the system offers the matching plan and tells you what it costs and when it will be charged. Nothing is charged at that moment: the new price applies from the next billing cycle.

What counts as an active employee?

Anyone marked active on the employees screen. Someone who has left and is marked inactive doesn't count toward the cap, and their history — shifts, hours and reports — stays where it is.

Why is there no price for chains?

From 71 employees up, things enter the picture that a price list can't hold: guided setup, migration from an existing system, and sometimes adjustments. The price is settled in a short call, and only then does a quote go out.

Can I export my data if I stop?

Always. Schedules, hours and employees export to Excel and CSV from inside the system, without asking us for anything.
